Epoch is the club's biggest event of the year: two days of challenges, workshops, a project showcase and **21 booths** — all running on **Epoch Coins** instead of cash.

## How the coins work

- **398 coins for everyone.** They land in your wallet when your ticket is checked at the entrance.
- **Spend them** at booths — from the VR Merge Zone to origami and digital art — and on merch like sticker packs, tees and hoodies.
- **Top up** at five recharge points: trivia, quick coding puzzles and more. Each one pays out once per person.
- **Your wallet is your phone.** Scan a booth's QR code, and the coins move. It still opens without signal.

## What's not decided yet

The exact dates in December and the ticket price — it may well be free. We'll post here and on Luma as soon as they're set, so [follow Epoch on Luma](https://luma.com/epoch-gitam-blr).
