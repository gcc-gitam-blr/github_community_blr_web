# Code of Conduct

The GitHub Community Club at GITAM University Bengaluru is for everyone — whether it's your
first day with Git or your thousandth commit. We want every event, chat and pull request to be
a place people feel welcome.

## We expect

- Kindness and patience, especially with beginners. Every expert was once a beginner.
- Respect for different backgrounds, identities, opinions and levels of experience.
- Constructive feedback on work — never personal attacks on people.
- Giving credit, and asking before sharing someone else's code, photos or details.

## We don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language, online or in person.
- Unwelcome attention, or sharing someone's private information.
- Disrupting events, talks or discussions on purpose.

## If something happens

Talk to any member of the core team at an event, or reach them privately through the club's
official channels. Reports are handled confidentially. Anyone asked to stop is expected to stop
immediately; the core team may remove people from events or club spaces.

This code applies at club events, in club chats and on this repository.
